# 01 · Project Brief

## Business Context

Carrefour is Europe's largest and the world's second-largest retailer. As of end-2025, the Group operates 15,719 stores worldwide, employs approximately 300,000 people, and runs directly in 7 countries: France, Spain, Belgium, Poland, Romania (Europe), Brazil, and Argentina (Latin America).

FY2025 net sales reached **€82.1 billion** (+2.8% like-for-like). E-commerce GMV grew 21% year-over-year to **€7.0 billion**, with a company target of €10 billion by 2026, backed by a planned **€3 billion digital investment**.

Carrefour is expanding AI across both the customer experience (Hopla, an OpenAI-powered shopping assistant) and store operations (Captana, a computer-vision shelf-monitoring system), alongside the Unlimitail joint venture (with Publicis Groupe) that uses first-party data from ~50 million loyalty members to build a high-margin retail media business.

*Source: Carrefour FY2025 Results Presentation (Feb 17, 2026) and Carrefour's public financial reports.*

## Problem Statement

There is a disconnect between Carrefour's traditional store model and its fast-growing digital/e-commerce arm — in operations, data, and customer experience. Revenue distribution across loyalty tiers (Basic, Silver, Gold, Platinum) is uneven, seasonal sales fluctuations aren't forecasted, and product-category performance isn't tracked consistently across store formats. In addition, returned orders were not being separated correctly in reporting, which can distort revenue figures.

## Project Objective

Build a data-driven decision-support system (dashboard and reporting infrastructure) reflecting regional sales performance, loyalty segments, and product-category revenue for Carrefour; document the current (As-Is) process; and propose a future (To-Be) digital solution. This simulates the full Business Analyst lifecycle — from discovery and requirements gathering, through process modeling, to acceptance criteria and KPI evaluation.

## Scope

| In Scope | Out of Scope |
|---|---|
| Regional / loyalty sales dashboard | Warehouse management system |
| Product category revenue analysis | Supply chain planning |
| Customer segmentation (loyalty tiers) | HR and recruitment processes |
| As-Is / To-Be process modeling (sales reporting) | Financial accounting systems |
| Requirements, business rules, acceptance criteria | Detailed marketing campaign management |
| KPI framework and dashboard evaluation | International tax and legal matters |

## Key Stakeholders (Summary)

Regional Sales Director, Data & Analytics team, E-commerce/Digital Transformation unit, Store Managers, Loyalty Program Owner, and Finance Controller. Full analysis in [Document 02](02-stakeholder-analysis.md).

## Success Criteria

- Regional and loyalty revenue in the dashboard refreshes within 24 hours.
- Returned orders are fully excluded from revenue calculations (0% error margin).
- Leadership can make near-real-time decisions instead of monthly.
- At least 80% of KPIs hit target within 2 quarters of initial rollout.
