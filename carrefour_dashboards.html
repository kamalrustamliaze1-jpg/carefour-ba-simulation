Key observations

  • Loyalty split: Basic customers generate the most revenue, with Platinum a close second. Gold sits lowest overall — a signal that warrants the cohort analysis below.
  • Seasonality: April and July form clear peaks, likely tied to seasonal campaigns or pre-holiday buying; September marks the year's low point.
  • Category mix: Dairy (19%), Bakery (17%) and Grocery (15%) carry the category split; Beverages is the smallest share at 8%.
  • Basket size: An average transaction of $104.47 is consistent with a standard supermarket-format basket.

Key observations

  • Margin over volume: Gold's 23% revenue margin is the strongest lens for comparing tiers — even with lower total revenue, Gold customers may be the most profitable to retain.
  • Bakery dominance: Bakery holds the top spot in both the overall dataset and the Gold segment (1,059 units, 23% of sales) — a consistent cross-tier pattern.
  • Runner-up categories: Fresh Produce (14%) and Dairy (12%) follow; Personal Care is the smallest at just 6%.
  • Open question: Explaining Gold's low overall revenue share requires a cohort view — customer count and visit frequency — flagged as a formal requirement (FR-04) in the report.
