# 10 · KPI & Evaluation

## Project-Level KPIs

| KPI | Baseline | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reporting delay | 2–3 weeks (manual) | ≤ 24 hours | Monthly |
| Data reconciliation accuracy | Unknown / unaudited | ≥ 99.5% (discrepancy < 0.5%) | Monthly |
| Dashboard load speed | N/A | < 3 seconds | Quarterly |
| User satisfaction (CSAT) | N/A | ≥ 4.2 / 5 | Quarterly |
| Returns error share in revenue | Unknown (mixed in) | 0% | Monthly |
| System uptime | N/A | ≥ 99.5% | Monthly |

## Business-Level KPIs (aligned to Carrefour's corporate targets)

| KPI | 2025 Actual | 2026 Target (Company announcement) |
|---|---|---|
| E-commerce GMV | €7.0 billion (+21%) | €10 billion |
| Private label share (food sales) | 38% | 40% |
| Retail media / data revenue (annual recurring) | Growth phase | > €1.5 billion (by 2030) |

| Digital investment | In execution | €3 billion (by 2026) |

*Source: Carrefour FY2025 Results Presentation and 2030 Digital Strategy announcements (Feb 2026).*

## Evaluation Method

Project success is assessed at quarterly review meetings against the KPIs above. Each KPI is assigned a red/yellow/green status: green = above 95% of target, yellow = 80–95%, red = below 80%. Yellow and red KPIs trigger root cause analysis and a corrective action plan.
